Who We Are

Crayola Design Studio is an architectural and interior design firm rooted in South India, operating across Bangalore, Trichy, Hyderabad, and Coimbatore.

Founded on the belief that great design is an act of listening, we approach every project as a collaboration. We understand your lifestyle, your aspirations, and the nuances of the spaces you inhabit — and we translate them into environments that are both deeply functional and visually extraordinary.

From a single room to an entire building facade, our team brings the same rigour, the same aesthetic sensibility, and the same respect for the client's vision to every brief.

Our Process

  • Consultation & Discovery

    We begin by listening. Understanding your lifestyle, requirements, and the fundamental purpose of the space is the foundation of everything we do.

  • Concept & ArchViz

    Ideas become photorealistic 3D visualizations. You walk through your space before a single brick is laid — experiencing light, texture, and scale firsthand.

  • Design Detailing

    We select materials, finalize color palettes, and create precise execution drawings. Every detail is resolved before work begins on site.

  • Execution & Styling

    The vision is brought to life through our network of skilled artisans and contractors, with our team present at every critical milestone.
